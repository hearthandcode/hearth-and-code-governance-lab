# Candidate Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Hearth & Code intends this project to be a welcoming, accessible, and technically rigorous place for contributors. Participation should make room for different bodies, identities, backgrounds, communication styles, access needs, experience levels, and ways of thinking.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss evidence, tradeoffs, and code without demeaning people.
- Respect consent, privacy, attribution, authorship, and governance boundaries.
- Make review feedback specific, actionable, and proportionate.
- Accommodate accessibility needs and avoid treating temporary confusion or unfinished work as personal deficiency.
- Preserve source material, uncertainty, and human decision gates rather than manufacturing agreement.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement gate

This applies to project repositories, issue trackers, review discussions, community spaces, and public representation of the project. No public enforcement contact or contribution venue exists yet. Public contribution is blocked until maintainers name a private reporting channel, response procedure, escalation route, and enforcement owners. Until then, preserve concerns privately and do not publish sensitive reports.

This candidate policy does not claim that a public community has launched or that Hearth & Code has accepted an enforcement obligation beyond the current private review surface.
